# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a respectful,
welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Supporting other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment and discrimination.
- Insulting or offensive comments.
- Personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing
our standards of acceptable behavior and taking appropriate action
when violations occur.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when
an individual officially represents the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed fairly
and confidentially.

## Enforcement Guidelines

1. Correction: A private warning explaining the violation.
2. Warning: A formal warning for unacceptable behavior.
3. Temporary Ban: Temporary exclusion from the community.
4. Permanent Ban: Permanent exclusion for serious or repeated
   violations.
